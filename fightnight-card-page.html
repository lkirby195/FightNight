How to read this page

Model % is the win probability from a rating-plus-statistics model trained only on fights that happened before this card. Fair is that probability written as a betting line with no vig. The movement bar shows where the market opened, where it is now, and where our fair line sits (red tick) — a solid bar means the market has moved toward our number since open.

CLV (closing line value) is the honest scoreboard: not "did we win the fight," but "did the market's final price move toward our published number." It cannot be faked after the fact, and it converges about ten times faster than win-loss records. Full methodology and the complete graded ledger, every pick since 2012, are public.

Straight talk

We do not beat the closing line, and no public picker credibly does. Our claim is narrower and proven: the model reaches the market's final answer days early. Debutants get no number — no UFC history, no projection. This page is analysis, not betting advice.

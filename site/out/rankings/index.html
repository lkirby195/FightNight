Reading the rating

Everyone starts at 1500. Around 1800 is a solid contender. 2000 and up is title territory: only a handful of fighters get there at a time.

We rank by the rating minus how unsure we are of it. A fighter who has fought often and recently gets full credit; a long layoff or a thin record costs a fighter places until the results come in.

Why Islam Makhachev is above Ciryl Gane

Ciryl Gane has the higher rating (2093 against 2090) but we're less sure of it: fewer or older results, so a wider margin of error (111 points against 95). Rating minus that margin puts Islam Makhachev first.

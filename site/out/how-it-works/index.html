How it works

One rating system, two rules, no exceptions

The rating

Every UFC fight since 1994 goes through the same rating system, in order, one at a time. A win over a strong opponent moves you up more than a win over a weak one; a finish counts for more than a split decision. Each fighter also carries a margin of error that shrinks with every bout and grows during a layoff. The rankings sort by rating minus that margin, so a fighter has to keep earning their place.

The pick

Before each card we combine the ratings with what each fighter has actually done in the cage (pace, finishes, takedowns, age, layoffs, reach) to put a number on every fight: our probability that each side wins. The number is fixed and posted before the fights happen, and never changed afterwards. Our pick is simply the side with the higher number.

The bet

Most weeks the odds and our numbers agree, and we do nothing. We bet only when they disagree in one of two specific ways, and the bar for each was fixed on 2026-09-08:

  • The underdog rule. The odds have a fighter as the underdog, but our number has that fighter winning at least 65% of the time.
  • The price rule. We agree with the favourite, but the odds are at least 100 points shorter than our number says they should be.

How much we bet was fixed on 2026-09-09: one unit ($100) when the fighter we're backing has a solid track record, sliding to half a unit as the record thins out. We don't bet at all when our fighter is a bigger underdog than +250 (more than 2.5-to-1 against), or when neither fighter has enough results to trust.

Keeping it honest

Picks and bets are posted before each event and timestamped. Every page on this site is generated from the public ledger and committed alongside it, so any change after a fight would be visible. Results are graded at the price that was on the board when the pick went up, not the opening line and not the closing line.

The numbers

Three things were frozen and dated, and none of them are tuned on results since:

  • Ratings engine and its parameters: frozen 2026-07-25.
  • Betting rules: frozen 2026-09-08.
  • Stake sizing: frozen 2026-09-09.

Across 3,571 fights from 2018–2026, when our number disagreed with the opening odds by 10 points, the closing odds moved about 2.4 points our way. Taken at the opening line and graded against the close, our picks averaged +2.26 points of closing-line value per fight.

Reading the ratings

Everyone starts at 1500. Around 1800 is a solid contender. 2000 and up is title territory: only a handful of fighters get there at a time.

We rank by net rating: the gross rating minus our confidence band, how far off the gross rating could be. A fighter who has fought often and recently gets full credit; a long layoff or a thin record costs a fighter places until the results come in.
